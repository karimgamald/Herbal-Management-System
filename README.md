### ✨ Key Features & AI Integration

* **🤖 AI-Powered Recipe Generation:**
  * **Personalized AI Recipes:** Generates tailored herbal recipes based strictly on the patient's updated Medical History to prevent adverse side effects.
  * **Interactive AI Chat Recipe:** Conversational AI assistant allowing users to query, customize, and receive instant herbal remedies.
  * **Expert Review:** Herbalists verify and approve AI-generated recommendations for safety assurance.

* **🌿 Herbs & Recipe Management:**
  * Comprehensive database of medicinal herbs, symptoms, dosage, and precautions.
  * Herbalists can catalog, edit, and link inventory directly with recipe creation.

* **🛒 Order & Supply Chain Management:**
  * Seamless ordering flow for patients to buy raw herbs or complete blended recipes.
  * Real-time order tracking and invoice/details view for buyers.
  * Herbalist dashboard for processing orders and tracking inventory depletion.

* **👨‍⚕️ Approval & Governance Workflow:**
  * Multi-tier approval system where Admins review and approve newly proposed herbs and disease definitions submitted by Herbalists.
